Questions

Frequently asked.

Is my entry fee tax-deductible?

Yes. Your team entry fee is a full donation to What Ability Foundation, an Australian registered charity. You will receive a tax-deductible receipt upon payment.

How many teams are available?

Corporate Games 2026 is strictly limited to 10 teams, 100 participants in total. Spots are allocated first-come, first-served.

Where are the venues?

All three venues, Archie Brothers, Hijinx Hotel and Strike Bowling, are Funlab-operated venues in Sydney. Exact addresses are confirmed in your registration pack.

What is included in the entry?

Full venue access across all three experiences, professional event facilitation, live scoring, an MC, light catering, trophies and fun awards, and your donation receipt.

Can we include a person with a disability in our team?

Absolutely, and we encourage it. Contact us to discuss how we can support inclusion within your team.

Who do I contact with questions?

Reach out to the What Ability Foundation team at hello@whatabilityfoundation.org.au and we'll get back to you promptly.
