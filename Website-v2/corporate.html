Corporate Partners

The national partners behind the Foundation.

National Partner

Cushman & Wakefield

"Spaces, and the people who shape them, can open doors for everyone."

One of the world's leading real estate services firms, Cushman & Wakefield's national partnership helps us reach more families living with disability across Australia, powering experiences that put happiness first.

Their commitment to inclusion extends beyond their business, and we're proud to have their team alongside us as we work toward our goal of one million experiences.

More about Cushman & Wakefield
National FUN Partner

Funlab

"Fun belongs to everyone."

The company behind Holey Moley, Strike, Archie Brothers and Hijinx Hotel is on a mission to make joy accessible for everyone: inclusion isn't just a value, it's built into everything they do.

Through Sensory Friendly Sessions, communication boards, sensory maps and discounted access for people living with disability at every venue, Funlab helps our participants be part of the fun, on equal terms, without compromise.

More about Funlab inclusiveness

Online & face to face Education & Engagement

'Let's Talk Inclusion' Workshops.

When you invest in creating disability confident employees, you'll see a change in attitude. Our tailored approach ensures that your team gains practical knowledge to create a workplace that values and supports individuals with diverse abilities.

  • ✓ Disability awareness and inclusion training sessions
  • ✓ Lived-experience storytelling to build empathy and understanding
  • ✓ Workshops on how to create more inclusive workplaces and customer experiences
  • ✓ Customise to your existing training formats as a "Lunch & Learn"
  • ✓ Tailored to your workplace goals

These sessions can be delivered online and face to face.
